### 🔍 Overview & Ecosystem Analysis

This repository tracks notable **commercial vaccine administration platforms**, **electronic health record (EHR) immunization modules**, and **open-source digital public goods** that manage immunization records, forecast vaccine schedules, track cold chain logistics, and orchestrate large-scale vaccination campaigns.

* **SaaS & Enterprise Market Leader Examples**: Salesforce Vaccine Cloud, Microsoft Cloud for Healthcare, Epic Systems, Cerner Immunization (Oracle Health), Athenahealth, VaxCare, Curative, and WellSky.
* **Open-Source & Digital Public Goods Emphasis**: Vaccine administration management is one of the strongest open-source domains in digital health. **HLN's Immunization Calculation Engine (ICE)** leads as the state-of-the-art open-source clinical decision support system. **OpenSRP2** provides an Electronic Immunization Register (EIR) for frontline health workers. **OpenLMIS** delivers GAVI-compliant vaccine supply chain tracking. **Sunbird RC** and **DIVOC** enable verifiable vaccination credentials at national scale. **ODK** serves as the global standard for offline data collection.

Contributions welcome! 🤝 Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## 🌐 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub Star Count (Descending)* 🌟

### 🚚 Supply Chain & Logistics

- **[ODK (Open Data Kit)](https://github.com/getodk/collect)**  <a href="https://github.com/getodk/collect/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/getodk/collect?style=social&color=white" alt="ODK Stars"/></a>  
  **Global standard for offline mobile data collection**, recognized as a Digital Public Good. Used extensively for vaccination monitoring, microplanning, counterfeit detection, vaccination status verification, and safety monitoring. Offline-first with barcode scanning, geographic data capture, and multimedia support. **Best for field data collection in immunization campaigns**.

- **[OpenLMIS](https://github.com/OpenLMIS/open-lmis)**  <a href="https://github.com/OpenLMIS/open-lmis/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OpenLMIS/open-lmis?style=social&color=white" alt="OpenLMIS Stars"/></a>  
  **Open-source electronic logistics management information system (LMIS)**, rated **"Fully Compliant" with GAVI's Target Software Standards**. Vaccine Module manages logistics processes at **10,000+ health facilities across 8 geographies**. Features cold chain equipment (CCE) inventory, temperature monitoring, vaccine stock management, and vial wastage tracking. **Best for vaccine supply chain management**.

### 📱 Client Engagement & Reminders

- **[RapidPro](https://github.com/rapidpro/rapidpro)**  <a href="https://github.com/rapidpro/rapidpro/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/rapidpro/rapidpro?style=social&color=white" alt="RapidPro Stars"/></a>  
  **UNICEF's open-source visual workflow engine for SMS and multi-channel communication**. Powers automated client-facing vaccination appointment reminders, immunization follow-up tracking, and public health awareness campaigns across frontline healthcare systems worldwide. **Best for automated vaccination messaging**.

### 🏥 Digital Health Information Systems

- **[DHIS2 Core](https://github.com/dhis2/dhis2-core)**  <a href="https://github.com/dhis2/dhis2-core/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/dhis2/dhis2-core?style=social&color=white" alt="DHIS2 Stars"/></a>  
  **Leading global health information platform** used by over 70 countries for public health management. Serves as a primary digital public good for tracking national immunization coverage rates, managing expanded program on immunization (EPI) dashboards, and analyzing vaccine safety analytics. **Best for national immunization health reporting**.

### 🆔 Master Patient Index & Interoperability

- **[OpenHIM (Open Health Information Mediator)](https://github.com/jembi/openhim-core-js)**  <a href="https://github.com/jembi/openhim-core-js/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/jembi/openhim-core-js?style=social&color=white" alt="OpenHIM Stars"/></a>  
  **Middleware for secure, standards-based health data exchange**. Facilitates interoperability between immunization systems including OpenSRP, DHIS2, and OpenLMIS utilizing HL7 FHIR and OpenHIE standards. **Best for health information exchange in immunization programs**.

- **[SantéMPI](https://github.com/santedb/santedb-server)**  <a href="https://github.com/santedb/santedb-server/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/santedb/santedb-server?style=social&color=white" alt="SantéMPI Stars"/></a>  
  **Master Patient Index/Client Registry platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed. Provides unique identity resolution for immunization data consolidation, harmonization, and cross-facility record matching. Supports OpenHIE, HL7 FHIR, and HL7v2. **Best for patient identity management in immunization systems**.

### 📋 Electronic Immunization Registries

- **[OpenSRP2 (FHIR Core)](https://github.com/opensrp/fhircore)**  <a href="https://github.com/opensrp/fhircore/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/opensrp/fhircore?style=social&color=white" alt="OpenSRP Stars"/></a>  
  **Open-source mobile health platform for frontline health workers**, Apache-2.0 licensed. Electronic Immunization Register (EIR) tracks individual immunization records, schedules follow-ups, and ensures adherence to vaccination schedules. Built on HL7 FHIR standards. **Best for community health worker immunization tracking**.

- **[Sunbird RC](https://github.com/Sunbird-RC/sunbird-rc-core)**  <a href="https://github.com/Sunbird-RC/sunbird-rc-core/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Sunbird-RC/sunbird-rc-core?style=social&color=white" alt="Sunbird RC Stars"/></a>  
  **Open-source framework for building electronic registries and issuing verifiable credentials**, part of the Sunbird Digital Public Good. Built for national scale with W3C Verifiable Credentials and instant QR code verification. **Best for vaccination credentialing and registries**.

- **[DIVOC (Digital Infrastructure for Verifiable Open Credentialing)](https://github.com/egovernments/divoc-docs)**  <a href="https://github.com/egovernments/divoc-docs/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/egovernments/divoc-docs?style=social&color=white" alt="DIVOC Stars"/></a>  
  **Open-source platform for large-scale health campaigns and verifiable certification**. Manages vaccines, facilities, and vaccinators systematically across geographies and generates digitally verifiable certificates compliant with international standards. **Best for national-scale vaccination campaign management**.

### ⚡ Clinical Decision Support & Forecasting

- **[HLN Immunization Calculation Engine (ICE)](https://github.com/HLNConsulting/ICE)**  <a href="https://github.com/HLNConsulting/ICE/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/HLNConsulting/ICE?style=social&color=white" alt="HLN ICE Stars"/></a>  
  **State-of-the-art open-source clinical decision support for immunizations**, Apache-2.0 licensed. Used in Immunization Information Systems (IIS), EHRs, and PHRs worldwide to provide vaccine forecasting logic adhering to ACIP recommendations. **The de facto open-source immunization forecaster**.

- **[imuGAP](https://github.com/ACCIDDA/imuGAP)**  <a href="https://github.com/ACCIDDA/imuGAP/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ACCIDDA/imuGAP?style=social&color=white" alt="imuGAP Stars"/></a>  
  **Bayesian hierarchical models of vaccine coverage by location, birth cohort, and age**, MIT licensed, published on CRAN. Implemented in Stan to decompose coverage into lifetime propensity and time-varying force of vaccination. **Best for vaccine coverage modeling and projection**.

- **[VCQI (Vaccination Coverage Quality Indicators)](https://github.com/ropensci/vcqiR)**  <a href="https://github.com/ropensci/vcqiR/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ropensci/vcqiR?style=social&color=white" alt="VCQI Stars"/></a>  
  **Flexible open-source tool for vaccination coverage survey data analysis**, developed by WHO and PAHO. Calculates indicators for access, coverage, continuity, and quality of vaccination for DHS, MICS, and EPI surveys. **Best for vaccination coverage survey analysis**.

### 📦 Community & Specialized Open-Source Repos

- **[Infant-Immunization-Manager](https://github.com/topics/vaccine-management)**  
  React/Node.js/MongoDB platform for healthcare providers to manage infant and maternal immunization schedules with automated notification alerts.
- **[Kids-Vaccination-Management-System](https://github.com/topics/immunization)**  
  Flask/MySQL web application for tracking pediatric vaccination histories, managing hospital dosage inventories, and generating compliance certificates.
- **[mVax (Duke University)](https://github.com/topics/vaccine-tracking)**  
  Routine immunization data collection suite created for rural clinics in Honduras featuring offline storage and peer-to-peer sync.
- **[immunify](https://github.com/topics/cold-chain)**  
  Open-source, country-owned framework for end-to-end immunization supply chain, cold-chain temperature telemetry monitoring, and vaccine stock replenishment.

## 🏗️ Integration Frameworks

**Building custom vaccine administration management solutions**:
* Combine **HLN ICE** for clinical decision support and vaccine forecasting.
* Use **OpenSRP2** for mobile electronic immunization registries (EIR) with offline capability.
* Deploy **OpenLMIS** for vaccine supply chain and cold-chain equipment management.
* Integrate **DIVOC** or **Sunbird RC** for cryptographically signed, verifiable vaccination certificates.
* Choose **ODK** for field data collection during active vaccination drives.
* Use **SantéMPI** for patient identity management across facilities.
* Integrate **VCQI** or **imuGAP** for post-campaign coverage analysis and Bayesian modeling.
